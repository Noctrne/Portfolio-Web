Kertas is a reading app for people who finish books slowly. The interface had grown
inconsistent across iOS, Android, and web, so the work started with a shared vocabulary
rather than new screens.

## System

- A fluid type scale that behaves the same on every platform
- Reading measure capped at 68 characters
- Three surface tones, no shadows
- Motion limited to page turns and sheet transitions

## Outcome

Figma libraries now map one-to-one to coded components, which cut the time to ship a new
screen from days to hours.
