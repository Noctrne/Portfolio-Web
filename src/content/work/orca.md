ORCA is the Gadjah Mada Robotic Team's autonomous underwater vehicle, built for the Singapore
Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Challenge. I joined the sub-team as a programmer in 2024 and
captained it through the 2026 cycle.

## Role

As captain I coordinated development across mechanical, electrical, and software work, and kept the
competition plan moving from technical review to pool time. On the software side I worked on sensor
handling, perception, and the mission behaviour that lets the vehicle complete its run without a
pilot.

## Result

ORCA finished in the Top 8 worldwide at SAUVC 2026. Leading the team two seasons after joining
taught me how much of engineering is coordination — making the vehicle understandable to the people
building it, not only functional in the water.
