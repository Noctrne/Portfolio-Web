A collection of mechanical design work in Fusion 360, kept here as a record of the parts that
support the software projects.

## Robotics arm

An arm study modelled around printed parts: a weighted base, two linkages, and a gripper at the
end. The exercise was less about the arm itself and more about understanding how load paths move
through a printed structure — where material belongs, and where it only looks strong.

## Mini 3D Scanner

The scanner's stepper housings, lifting mechanism, spin plate, and limiters were designed alongside
the electronics, so the printed parts fit the motors and sensor rather than the other way around.
