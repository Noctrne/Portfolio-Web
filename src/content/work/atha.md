Atha makes clothing in small runs, mostly to order, mostly in natural fibres. The brand
needed to look considered without looking expensive — closer to a workshop than a boutique.

## Direction

The system is built on a single arch, a hairline rule, and a palette pulled from undyed
fabric. Nothing is decorative for its own sake; every element has a job.

## What was delivered

- Wordmark and monogram
- Editorial grid for lookbooks and packaging
- Product photography direction
- A five-page website with a simple catalogue

The identity now runs across tags, tissue paper, and the studio's site without needing a
separate set of rules for each.
