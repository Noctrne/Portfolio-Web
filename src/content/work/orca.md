ORCA V3 is the Gadjah Mada Robotic Team's autonomous underwater vehicle, built for the
Singapore Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Challenge. I led the sub-team through the 2026 cycle,
from technical planning to competition execution.

## Role

As captain I coordinated the vehicle's development across mechanical, electrical, and software
work. As a programmer I worked on the software stack — sensor handling, perception, and the
mission behaviour that lets the vehicle complete its run without a pilot.

## Result

ORCA V3 finished in the Top 8 worldwide at SAUVC 2026. The same programme is where I started as
a programmer in 2024; leading it two seasons later meant learning how much of engineering is
coordination.
