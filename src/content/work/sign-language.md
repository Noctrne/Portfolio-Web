Sign language is spatial, fast, and full of information that a single camera frame cannot hold.
Our team project explored how computer vision could translate gestures into text.

## What we explored

The project combined hand detection with sequence modelling, then tested how well the system held
up across different signing speeds and camera angles. Working within a competition deadline made
scope discipline part of the engineering.

## Context

Entered in the Intel AI Global Impact Festival 2023 with a team from Yogyakarta.
