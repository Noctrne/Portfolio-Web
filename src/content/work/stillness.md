This series began with a narrow window and a wall that turned the colour of honey for
about forty minutes each day. I started photographing it without a plan, then kept going
until the light changed with the season.

## Approach

Everything was made in the same two rooms, with one lens and no artificial light. The
constraint was the point: instead of chasing new subjects, I waited for familiar ones to
become unfamiliar again.

- Natural light only, between 3pm and 5pm
- One 35mm equivalent lens
- No cropping in post — the frame is the composition

> The room did not change. The light did.

## Notes

The edit moves from wide, quiet views toward details — a cup, a folded cloth, the edge of
a doorway — before returning to the window where the series started. Twenty-two frames
were selected from just over six hundred.
