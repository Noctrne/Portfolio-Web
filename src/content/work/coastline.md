For one year I returned to the same stretch of coast and made a single frame each visit.
The project is less about the sea than about repetition — what changes when nothing moves.

## Method

A fixed position, marked with a stone. A fixed focal length. The only variables were time,
tide, and weather, which turned out to be enough.

## Selection

Nine frames are shown here, arranged chronologically. Two are almost identical, six months
apart, which is the point.
