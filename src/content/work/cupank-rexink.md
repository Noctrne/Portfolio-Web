Cupank Rexink is an autonomous underwater vehicle developed by the robotics division of Elins
Research Club UGM. I joined as a programmer; because of my experience in the GMRT sub-team, I also
ended up advising the team on software structure and pool testing.

## Work

I built the Gazebo simulation environment used to test behaviour before pool time, and worked on a
custom firmware build for the flight controller. Keeping the simulation close to the real vehicle
meant the team could rehearse missions without waiting for a slot in the pool.

## Links

The simulation, firmware, and the wider project repositories are public. They document the vehicle
model, pool course, and the ROS 2 packages used on the vehicle.
