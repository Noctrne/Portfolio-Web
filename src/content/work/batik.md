Batik has hundreds of named motifs, each with regional and cultural meaning. This project
explored whether a computer-vision model could identify a pattern from a photograph.

## Approach

The work started with collecting and cleaning motif images, then training a classifier and
testing it against patterns it had not seen. The interesting part was not the accuracy number but
the failure cases — motifs that share a structure often share a name.

## Context

Built as part of the student exchange programme at Singapore ITE College, alongside an IoT
watering system and a Yogyakarta chatbot.
