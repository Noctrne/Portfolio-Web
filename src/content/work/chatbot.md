A small chatbot that answers questions about Yogyakarta — places, food, and how to get between
them. Built during the exchange programme at Singapore ITE College.

## Scope

The project focused on a narrow, useful set of local knowledge rather than open-ended
conversation, with fallbacks that admit when the bot does not know something.

## What I took from it

Conversation is an interface with no visible layout. Writing the responses was as much editorial
work as engineering — the same instinct I use in photography and design.
